Meet Sonjii, your practitioner at Heavenly Faces Aesthetics, where personal consultation guides every skincare recommendation and treatment discussion.

Sonjii brings over 30 years of healthcare experience to Heavenly Faces Aesthetics, spanning nursing, midwifery, public health and screening, supported by further aesthetic training courses.

Based in Barkingside, Ilford, Heavenly Faces Aesthetics offers individual consultations, chemical peels, Dermapen microneedling, dermal fillers and skin booster options. Sonjii explains suitability, preparation, possible risks and aftercare so you can consider your choices without pressure.
